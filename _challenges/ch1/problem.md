## Challenge 1 — Moving Target

Build a tiny Canvas program containing **one square**.

### Rules

- The square starts somewhere near the center of the canvas.
- The player can press the **arrow keys**.
- `↑` moves the square up.
- `↓` moves it down.
- `←` moves it left.
- `→` moves it right.
- The square should move at a constant speed.
- The square must **never leave the canvas**.

That's it.

### Important

Don't worry about making it pretty. No game architecture, classes, fancy abstractions, etc.

I'm specifically interested in **how you think about the problem**:

- What state do you need?
- How do you represent the square's position?
- How do you decide where it can and can't move?
- How do keyboard input and movement interact?

**Write the whole thing yourself.** If you don't remember a Canvas API call, ask me for that specific API knowledge rather than looking for the solution to the problem.

When you're finished, send me your code. I'll evaluate how you approached it and then give you **Challenge 2** based on your actual performance.
